About

The music of AmberTunes — stories told without words.

Stylist and photograph — P. Boroduln

AmberTunes is the musical world of composer Maxim Novak — a space where cinematic sound meets the warmth of jazz, bossa nova, and nostalgia.

His work has been featured in documentary films, artistic collaborations, and independent media projects around the world. Every piece carries a sense of narrative — a quiet reflection, a dialogue between melody and image.

Maxim’s compositions often explore retro moods, emotional storytelling and the intersection of memory, time, and place. They are written for listeners who appreciate subtlety, intimacy, and beauty in simplicity.

Whether it’s a city at dusk or a silent countryside road — AmberTunes captures the feeling of being there, in that fragile moment.
